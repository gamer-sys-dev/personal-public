        Progress

          Rule of thumb: short sessions of 5 to 10 reps. When he gets 80% or more in three sessions running, make it a bit harder (more distance, longer duration or more distraction). If he drops below 50%, go back a step.

            Weight

              Body score matters more than the number on the scales, especially for crosses. You should feel his ribs easily under a thin layer, see a waist from above and a tuck behind the ribs from the side. Puppies vary hugely, so watch the trend and ask your vet if gain stalls or drops.
